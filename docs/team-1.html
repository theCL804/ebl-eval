Where They Stand

This is the most decorated franchise in league history, four straight championships from 2020 through 2023, a run nobody else in this league has come close to matching. Since then: no finals appearance in 2024, and a 5-9 finish in 2025. That is as sharp a fall from a dynasty as this league has seen.

Finished 5-9 in 2025, tied for the worst record in the league by wins, but the underlying numbers tell a different story: 1290.68 points scored actually outpaces four teams that made the playoffs. That gap between record and output usually closes on its own without a full rebuild being necessary. Heading into this draft they sit in the middle of the pack in draft capital but comfortably in the top half in roster talent, the classic sign of a former champion a couple of lineup tweaks away from the playoff mix rather than one that needs to blow anything up.

Future Outlook

The receiver room isn't just deep, it's young: Pickens (25), Olave (26), and Downs (24) are all entering their prime rather than leaving it, and Herbert doesn't turn 30 until 2028. That's a three-year runway, not a one-year fix, and it lines up well with when the 2028 draft capital (five picks, including a first) is scheduled to land.

The name to watch is Oronde Gadsden at TE, a 23-year-old who's the only real answer at the position right now; if he develops into a legitimate starter over the next two seasons, this roster stops needing to draft TE at all and can pour every future pick into fixing running back instead.

Scouting Report

The Chicken Boys have the best pure receiver room in the league not named Moon Unit, and Herbert gives them a real quarterback anchor. The 2025 fade to 5-9 looks more like touchdown luck evening out than a talent problem, since almost nothing about this roster got worse. The draft capital here is the interesting part: a 2026 first plus three fourths, and then a genuinely stacked 2028 with two firsts. That is a retooling team's dream position, not a rebuilder's. Use the 2026 first on the best true bell-cow back available, since RB is the one spot where name recognition doesn't match production, and stop paying for WR depth you already have.

Strengths

  • Herbert at QB with four full seasons of team control left
  • WR room is genuinely deep: Waddle, Pickens, Olave, Downs as the four, with Golden and Higgins developing behind them
  • Four picks in the 2026 rookie draft including the 1.0x, plus a loaded 2028 (five picks, two firsts)

Weaknesses

  • TE is a black hole beyond Gadsden
  • RB2 behind Pollard/Walker is a pile of dice rolls (Charbonnet on PUP, a stack of rookie-year backs)
  • Record cratered from 10-4 to 5-9 without much roster change, which usually means touchdown variance more than a real collapse

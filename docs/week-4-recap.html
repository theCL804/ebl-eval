Week 4: Upset Week: All Three Unbeatens Go Down

Moon Unit, BateBoul, and DickTatorMiller all walked into week 4 at 3-0. All three walked out 3-1, two of them beaten by teams that hadn't won a game all season. Five teams are now bunched at 3-1, and the league suddenly looks wide open instead of top-heavy.

The Chicken Boys🐔 vs The Choo Choo Crew

Scores

The Chicken Boys🐔 134.9
The Choo Choo Crew 81.7

Margin: 53.2

Chicken Boys moved to 3-1 with the week's biggest blowout, 134.94 to 81.72. Kenneth Walker (31.4) and Nico Collins (28.3) did the damage, with Tyler Shough (16.94) starting at quarterback over a healthy-looking Justin Herbert, who put up 12.56 on the bench, close enough that it reads as a real season-long decision rather than a panic move. Choo Choo Crew falls to 0-4, and this one wasn't close to unlucky: Joe Burrow had a strong game (25.72) and Jonathan Taylor added 22.2, but they also started Courtland Sutton (0.9) in the flex while Tyquan Thornton sat on the bench with 27.6, a swap worth 26.7 points on its own. It wouldn't have been enough to win this particular blowout, but after two weeks of looking like a good team with bad luck, this is now a team that's lost four in a row and is also leaving its own best players on the bench.

DickTatorMiller 80.8
The Factory 85.2

Margin: 4.4

The Factory's first signature week of the season, and it came at the best possible time: a win over a 3-0 DickTatorMiller team. Kyle Monangai ran for 27.5 and Bo Nix added 13.56 at quarterback, modest numbers that were enough because DickTatorMiller's lineup fell apart around the edges. C.J. Stroud led them with 25.08, but they started Rashee Rice (0.0) at receiver while Brian Thomas sat on the bench with 8.7, and started Travis Kelce (2.5) at tight end while Michael Mayer sat on the bench with 12.2. Either swap alone, worth 8.7 and 9.7 points respectively, would have been enough to turn an 85.16 to 80.78 loss into a win. Three weeks of being the league's quiet unbeaten team ends here, and not just because of bad luck.

The Cheese Children 143.6
The Moon Unit 139.0

Margin: 4.6

Cheese Children beat the best team in the league, and for once their own front office deserves real credit, mostly. Tetairoa McMillan exploded for 38.2, Puka Nacua bounced back with 23.2 after weeks of sitting on the bench scoring nothing, and Bryce Young started at quarterback and delivered 21.46, the correct call after three straight weeks of starting the wrong QB. Moon Unit still put up 139.0, with CeeDee Lamb (32.8) and Bijan Robinson (30.2) leading the way, and their bench alone would have been a top-half lineup by itself: Jared Goff (22.48), Keon Coleman (20.60) and T.J. Hockenson (18.40) all sat unused, over 61 combined points this roster simply didn't need to call on. That's the gap between the two teams right now, Moon Unit can lose a close one and still have that much in reserve. The asterisk on Cheese Children's win: they also started Jordan Addison (7.1) and Christian Watson (6.2) over Romeo Doubs (20.8) and Malik Nabers (20.2), both sitting unused on the bench. That's roughly 27 points left on the table at receiver, a fourth straight week with a real lineup mistake. It just didn't cost them this time, because the quarterback decision alone was worth enough to flip the game, 143.56 to 139.0.

Fuck Michael Thomas 83.3
Long Live Wopo 90.8

Margin: 7.5

Long Live Wopo moved to 2-2 behind a 28.8-point Javonte Williams game and 19.88 from Lamar Jackson. Fuck Michael Thomas got a modest floor from Ashton Jeanty (15.6) and Jordan Love (14.08) but Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who'd gone for 41.0 and 30.36 the two weeks prior, came back down to 10.1, and without that outlier game this team just doesn't have enough juice yet, falling to 2-2.

Mattpao 113.6
BateBoul 112.0

Margin: 1.6

This is the upset of the season so far, and it turns out it wasn't a pure fluke on BateBoul's part either. BateBoul came in 3-0 with the second-best point differential in the league, and lost to a Mattpao team that was 1-2 and hadn't shown much all year. Kyren Williams put up 31.7 and Dak Prescott added 19.1, a perfectly good week by any normal measure, but BateBoul also started Cam Skattebo (7.1) in the flex while Brian Robinson sat on the bench with 25.2, a direct running-back-for-running-back swap worth 18.1 points on its own, more than enough by itself to have flipped this result. Mattpao, meanwhile, got exactly the week they needed: 25.96 from Aaron Rodgers, 25.4 from Chuba Hubbard, 20.3 from Alvin Kamara, and 15.9 from Roman Wilson, four players all hitting at once. BateBoul outscored at the position that mattered and still found a way to hand this one over.

Brock Hard 117.7
Nikkis’ Victims 116.4

Margin: 1.3

The week's closest game. Brock Hard improved to 3-1 behind Zay Flowers (22.8) and Brock Purdy (22.62), surviving Nikki's Victims by 1.3 points. Josh Allen had a solid 20.52 but Emanuel Wilson actually led the Victims' lineup with 25.5, and Tee Higgins added 21.2, a genuinely balanced effort that still came up just short at 116.42 to 117.72.

Where They Stand

Trending in the right direction, up from 7-7 to 8-6 with the second-most points scored in the league in 2025 (1418.74), trailing only the three teams that finished with 11 or more wins. Real production without a gaudy win total usually means better health or better in-season decisions are the difference between this team and the top tier.

Right now they sit one clear tier below BateBoul, the Choo Choo Crew, and the Moon Unit, but with the deepest bench in the league and a full run of picks through 2028, they have both the assets and the timeline to close that gap as soon as this offseason rather than needing multiple years to get there.

Future Outlook

This is one of the youngest elite cores in the league: Nabers (23), Burden (22), and McMillan (23) are all still on rookie deals and still improving, and Judkins (22) gives them a running back with the same long runway. None of that talent is going anywhere for years.

Combined with a full complement of picks in every round through 2028, this team doesn't have a ceiling problem, it has an allocation problem: as long as future capital gets spent fixing QB and TE instead of adding to an already-loaded WR room, this roster should be a top-two or top-three team for the rest of the decade.

Scouting Report

The Cheese Children have built the kind of roster you get by consistently drafting and developing well rather than chasing names: four legitimate starting-caliber receivers with three more capable of starting elsewhere. That WR surplus is the asset to spend. This team doesn't need another receiver, it needs to convert bench depth at the position into a real difference-maker at QB or TE, where the room is currently replacement level once you get past Likely. A trade sending out two of Meyers/Doubs/Watson for a top-12 TE or a young ascending QB would turn a quietly good roster into the best team in the league.

Strengths

  • WR corps is absurd: Nabers, Burden, McMillan, Nacua starting, with Meyers, Smith, Doubs, Pierce, Watson still on the bench
  • Improved from 7-7 to 8-6 with a young core that's still ascending
  • Full complement of picks every round through 2028

Weaknesses

  • TE production is thin even though the room is crowded (Likely as the lead option, Kittle on PUP at 32)
  • QB is Mayfield/Smith/Young, serviceable but not a difference-maker
  • So much WR depth that some of it is genuinely wasted on a bench that can't play more than four

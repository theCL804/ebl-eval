Where They Stand

11-3 in 2025, the second-best record and second-most points in the league (1626.14), a clear step up from an already solid 8-6 mark in 2024, and this team turned that into the franchise's first championship, beating BateBoul in the final. That two-year upward trajectory has now arrived at a title, not just contention.

Only two other rosters scored more points in 2025, and the gap from that top tier down to the rest of the league is wide. The real question for this team isn't whether it's good enough to win now, it clearly is, it's how long the window stays open given the injury risk sitting at the center of the roster in CMC.

Future Outlook

Bijan Robinson (24) is the one true long-term answer on this roster, and he's already good enough to be a team's clear lead back the day CMC's workload starts getting managed down. St. Brown (26) also has several prime years left. Past those two, though, the rest of the core (Mahomes, CMC, A.J. Brown, Lamb) is aging as a group.

The thin draft capital (just a third and fourth this year, a first, third, and fourth in both 2027 and 2028) means there isn't much coming to replace that group when it goes. The realistic outlook is a two-to-three-year win-now window built around Bijan and St. Brown, with CMC's trade value the best tool available to buy insurance before that window narrows.

Scouting Report

The Moon Unit paid for this roster with future picks and it's hard to argue with the result: the best record in the league and a starting lineup with almost no weak link. The one real risk is CMC, who is entering the danger zone for a running back with his mileage, and there is no obvious in-house replacement if he falls off. The 2026 draft is thin here on purpose, so the move isn't to force a rookie pick, it's to use CMC's remaining trade value now, while he's still productive, to bring back a younger complementary piece before the market prices in the decline everyone can see coming.

Strengths

  • Mahomes, CMC, Bijan, A.J. Brown, CeeDee Lamb, St. Brown as the starting eight is as good as it gets in this league
  • 11-3 in 2025, the league's best record, and it wasn't particularly close
  • TE room is deep enough to survive an injury (LaPorta, Hockenson, Ferguson all rostered)

Weaknesses

  • Only two 2026 picks (a third and a fourth), clearly the cost of building this roster
  • CMC turns 31 this season and is coming off a 400-touch workload, a profile that tends to break down fast
  • Very little young young talent behind the stars if age catches up all at once

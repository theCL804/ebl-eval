Where They Stand

Two years of losing seasons, but the trajectory is actually upward: 3-11 in 2024 improved to 6-8 in 2025, and scoring ticked up too (953.06 to 1059.84), even though both marks still sit near the bottom of the league. It's a long way removed from this franchise's high point, a championship all the way back in 2018, but the rebuild has clearly been underway for a while and the draft capital reflects it.

That combination, more losses than almost anyone but a front office that has clearly been trading proven production for future capital, points to a rebuild that is further along than the record suggests. Sitting on three firsts and three seconds in this class alone, they have more draft capital than current production, which is exactly the state a rebuild should be in a year or two before it's supposed to contend again.

Future Outlook

Jeanty and Loveland are both 22 and both entering situations where their target and touch share should only grow, and Xavier Worthy (23) gives this team a legitimate third foundational piece if his role keeps expanding. None of the three turns 25 until at least 2028, so the core doesn't need replacing again anytime soon.

That's the real case for patience here: this isn't a team hoping the draft finds its stars, the stars are already on the roster and just need two more years to mature. The picks are for filling in around them, not replacing them, which is the healthiest possible position for a team that's currently losing.

Scouting Report

This is a team that has clearly been selling for two years, and the return is sitting right there: three firsts and three seconds in this rookie class alone. Jeanty and Loveland are the kind of foundational pieces a rebuild is supposed to produce, and now the job is to keep stacking. With this much draft capital, the temptation is to take the top player at every pick, but the better play is package two or three of these seconds to jump back into the first round and guarantee a second blue-chip rookie. Stafford is a one-year QB rental at best, don't build around him. Sit through one more mediocre season, spend every pick you have on this class and the next one, and this becomes the scariest team in the league by 2028.

Strengths

  • Three first-round picks in the 2026 draft plus three more seconds, the single biggest draft haul in the league this year
  • Jeanty and Loveland give this team two blue-chip rookies to build around already
  • Full four-round complement in both 2027 and 2028 on top of it

Weaknesses

  • Stafford at 38 as the starting QB is a bridge to nowhere
  • Bench is full of players no longer on NFL rosters (Ekeler, McCoy, Michael Thomas)
  • Back-to-back losing seasons (3-11, 6-8) with declining scoring both years

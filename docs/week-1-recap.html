Week 1: Retool Beats Rebuild, Cheese Children Beat Themselves

Week 1 mostly went to form: the Win-Now Contenders (Moon Unit, Brock Hard, BateBoul, Choo Choo Crew) went a combined 3-1, and both preseason Rebuild teams (The Factory, Nikki's Victims) lost, one of them embarrassingly. But the real story of the week wasn't a Rebuild team losing, it was a Contender doing it to themselves.

The Chicken Boys🐔 vs The Factory

Scores

The Chicken Boys🐔 128.4
The Factory 66.4

Margin: 61.9

This wasn't a game, it was a formality. The Factory came in with the most 2026 draft capital in the league and a roster that hasn't been built around it yet, and week 1 made the case for the rebuild louder than any scouting report could: nobody on the roster topped Bucky Irving's 16.8, starting QB Drake Maye managed 12.82, and the entire eight-man lineup combined for less than what Chicken Boys got out of Kenneth Walker (35.6) and Chris Olave (25.2) alone. Chicken Boys, for their part, got the kind of balanced production a "fallen-off dynasty" isn't supposed to have this early: Walker, Olave, Isaiah Likely (23.8) and Nico Collins (17.7) all hit mid-teens or better.

DickTatorMiller 131.0
Long Live Wopo 115.5

Margin: 15.5

DickTatorMiller led the entire league in scoring this week and there's a real chance nobody noticed, because a Retool team quietly posting 130 behind a 34.8-point Derrick Henry game doesn't make headlines the way a contender collapsing does. DJ Moore (21.5) and Jayden Daniels (17.66) rounded out a genuinely deep effort. Long Live Wopo got 29.96 from Lamar Jackson and 27.2 from Justin Jefferson, two stars doing exactly what they're supposed to do, and it still wasn't enough against a full lineup clicking at once.

The Cheese Children 53.7
Fuck Michael Thomas 96.4

Margin: 42.7

This is the one to talk about. Cheese Children came into the week labeled a Contender by every preseason measure, "the deepest, most balanced roster in the league," and then somehow posted the week's low score while their own bench outscored their own starting lineup at two separate positions. Baker Mayfield started at QB and put up 11.64; Bryce Young sat on their bench and put up 36.44. Jordan Addison started at WR and scored a flat 0.0; Christian Watson sat behind him and scored 32.70. Between those two swaps alone, this team left over 61 points on the bench in a game they lost by 42.66. This is also, for the record, the same franchise that hung 167.3 on The Factory in the single biggest blowout in league history back in 2021. Nobody's questioning the roster. Somebody needs to question the lineup card. Meanwhile Fuck Michael Thomas, a preseason Rebuild team, got a legitimate breakout from rookie Ashton Jeanty (29.7) and a strong Jaxon Smith-Njigba game (25.2) and won going away without needing much from the rest.

The Moon Unit 124.0
Mattpao 93.2

Margin: 30.8

Caleb Williams put up 38.26, the second-best individual score of the week by anyone, and it still wasn't close, because Moon Unit doesn't have one guy who goes off, it has four: Bijan Robinson (27.3), Trevor Lawrence (26.1), Amon-Ra St. Brown (23.7) and CeeDee Lamb (12.9) all cleared double digits. Mattpao's problem wasn't Williams, it was everyone else: Chuba Hubbard (22.2) was fine, but the next five players combined for under 33 points behind a rookie-heavy WR room (Emeka Egbuka, Rome Odunze) that isn't producing consistently yet.

Brock Hard 123.6
The Choo Choo Crew 117.7

Margin: 5.9

The week's closest game, and it played out exactly like two Win-Now Contenders beating on each other should. Zay Flowers (27.5) and David Montgomery (27.4) carried Brock Hard, while D'Andre Swift went off for 31.9 and nearly single-handedly dragged Choo Choo Crew to the upset before Jonathan Taylor's 23.6 wasn't quite enough backup.

BateBoul 119.3
Nikkis’ Victims 87.1

Margin: 32.3

Nikki's Victims talked a big game all week and then needed 39.66 points out of Josh Allen, the league's resident handout quarterback, just to keep this from getting ugly. Strip Allen out and the rest of the roster combined for under 50 points, with Tee Higgins (7.4), Marvin Harrison (3.8) and Jayden Reed (3.5) doing essentially nothing. BateBoul didn't need the theatrics: Jahmyr Gibbs (31.1) and Jalen Hurts (28.72) did the heavy lifting before Trey McBride tacked on another 20, and this "back-to-back dominant, no championship yet" team looked like exactly that again.

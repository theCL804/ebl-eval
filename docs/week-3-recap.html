Week 3: The Lucky Run Ends, and BateBoul Looks Like the Team to Beat

Three teams are 3-0 now (BateBoul, The Moon Unit, DickTatorMiller), and one of them just put up the single best week anyone's had all season. The Chicken Boys' charmed 2-0 start finally caught up with them. Cheese Children found a new way to mismanage their own roster, their third different quarterback mistake in three different weeks. And for the first time, Josh Allen had a normal game, and Nikki's Victims lost anyway.

Scores

The Chicken Boys🐔 100.1
The Moon Unit 119.6

Margin: 19.5

The warning signs from the last two weeks finally cashed in: Chicken Boys lost for the first time, dropping to 2-1. They also turned to Tyler Shough at quarterback instead of Justin Herbert, and Shough actually led the team with 24.8, with Kenneth Walker (20.3) and Bhayshul Tuten (16.0) behind him, but it wasn't enough against a Moon Unit team that just keeps doing this: Bijan Robinson went for 35.3, Christian McCaffrey added 19.6, and Patrick Mahomes chipped in 17.94 at quarterback. The Moon Unit are 3-0, and nothing about how they're winning looks fluky.

DickTatorMiller 97.2
The Choo Choo Crew 74.3

Margin: 22.9

DickTatorMiller is 3-0 and still the quietest unbeaten team in the league. Derrick Henry led the way again with 21.4, Travis Kelce added 13.9, and C.J. Stroud got the start at quarterback for 11.68. Choo Choo Crew dropped to 0-3 despite a strong Joe Burrow game (22.58); the supporting cast just isn't there yet, with Parker Washington's 11.5 as the next-best score on the roster.

The Cheese Children 112.1
BateBoul 157.4

Margin: 45.3

BateBoul put up 157.42, the best single week by anyone so far this season, and it came from everywhere: Jahmyr Gibbs went for 37.9, Drake London 25.9, Ja'Marr Chase 20.3, Kyren Williams 18.8, and Deebo Samuel 17.4. That's five separate players over 17 points in one lineup. Cheese Children, for what it's worth, actually played a good game on their end, 112.14, their best output of the season, and it still wasn't within 40 points. They also found a new way to mess up their own lineup: after benching Bryce Young behind Baker Mayfield for two straight weeks, they finally started Young (14.64) over Mayfield (benched, 15.28, basically a coin flip either way) -- and then left Geno Smith, their third rostered quarterback, on the bench with 27.04 points, the best QB output on the entire team, unused. Three weeks, three different quarterbacks mishandled. This team cannot get out of its own way at that position.

Fuck Michael Thomas 107.1
Nikkis’ Victims 94.2

Margin: 13.0

For the first time all season, Josh Allen had a normal game, 18.96, nowhere near his 43.82 and 39.66 from the first two weeks, and Nikki's Victims lost anyway, falling to 1-2. Harold Fannin (20.6) and Tee Higgins (18.0) tried to pick up the slack but it wasn't enough. On the other side, Jaxon Smith-Njigba is turning into a real problem for people's bye weeks and waiver boards both: 30.36 this week after a 41.0 the week before, back-to-back monster games for a team that's now 2-1 and starting to look like it was never really a Rebuild team to begin with. Jordan Love added 20.48 and Michael Wilson a surprising 20.4 behind him.

Mattpao 75.2
The Factory 64.2

Margin: 11.0

The week's closest game, and also the week's lowest-scoring one. The Factory is 0-3 and still hasn't had a single week that looks like anything, this time managing just 64.16 behind Matthew Golden (19.5) and Wan'Dale Robinson (15.2), with Drake Maye managing only 5.76 at quarterback. Mattpao escaped with 75.18 on the back of a 24.68 Aaron Rodgers start, enough to move to 1-2, but this wasn't exactly must-see football for either side.

Brock Hard 108.9
Long Live Wopo 83.1

Margin: 25.7

Brock Hard moved to 2-1 behind another big Brock Purdy game (34.28) plus a strong two-way effort from Brock Bowers (22.6) and Garrett Wilson (21.7). Long Live Wopo got a solid floor from Lamar Jackson (20.44) and Davante Adams (18.2) but didn't have enough behind them, falling to 1-2.

Where They Stand

The definition of consistent: 8-6 in both 2024 and 2025, with respectable point totals both years (1578.0, then 1473.36) that comfortably clear the bottom half of the league. Not a top-three team by regular-season record right now, but this is the only franchise in the league with multiple championships, winning it in 2019 and again in 2024, plus a runner-up finish in 2022. Three finals appearances in the league's history and this is the team in two of them.

With a full run of picks and no glaring roster need, this is a team that could climb into the top tier simply by drafting well and letting Dart continue to develop, without needing a single dramatic move to get there.

Future Outlook

Dart is only 23 and already the starter, and if he develops the way rookie QBs with his draft capital typically do, this team has a true long-term answer at the league's most important position, something more than half the league can't say. Javonte Williams (26) and Jonathon Brooks (23) give the backfield real depth beyond Barkley's timeline too.

With a full run of picks through 2027, this team doesn't need to make a big swing, it just needs to keep drafting well and let Dart's development set the pace. If he hits, Long Live Wopo has as good a multi-year outlook as anyone not already in the league's top tier.

Scouting Report

Long Live Wopo doesn't have a glaring weakness, which is its own kind of advantage in a league with this much roster turnover elsewhere. Barkley, Jacobs, and Jefferson form a stable core, and having a full run of picks through 2027 means this team can be patient and opportunistic rather than desperate. The swing factor is Dart: if he takes a real second-year step, this roster has no obvious ceiling. Use this year's picks on WR depth rather than reaching for need, since the roster construction here rewards adding the best player available and letting the lineup sort itself out.

Strengths

  • Barkley, Jacobs, and Javonte Williams give this team three legitimate every-down-caliber backs
  • Jefferson at WR1 is as safe a foundational piece as exists in fantasy
  • Full complement of picks in 2026 and 2027, so nothing needs to be forced

Weaknesses

  • Dart is a rookie QB with all the associated volatility
  • Depth behind the big three names thins out fast at WR
  • 8-6 two years running is good, not great, in a league with multiple 12-2 teams

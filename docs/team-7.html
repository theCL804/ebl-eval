Where They Stand

Fell from a strong 10-4 season in 2024 to 6-8 in 2025, and unlike some of the other decliners in this league, the point totals fell hard too, from 1518.52 down to 1146.16. This wasn't just touchdown variance evening out, the offense actually got worse. It's a familiar shape for this franchise: finals losses in 2018 and 2020 mean this is the only team in the league to reach the championship game twice without ever winning it.

That decline, combined with zero picks anywhere in this draft to correct course, makes this the most precarious standing in the league heading into the season: a team that was recently good, is trending the wrong way, and has no draft-capital cushion if 2026 goes sideways as well.

Future Outlook

Brock Bowers is a 23-year-old who's already a top-two dynasty TE, and Garrett Wilson (26) and Zay Flowers (25) both still have several prime years ahead. That trio alone is a real long-term foundation, arguably as good as any three-player core in the league at their positions.

The problem is everything around them: Evans is 32 and won't be part of the next contention window, and with almost no picks anywhere through 2028 (a second in 2027, a first, second, and fourth in 2028), there's very little way to replace him or the aging running back room. The outlook here hinges entirely on aggressive trades, since the draft isn't going to be the tool that fixes this roster.

Scouting Report

Brock Hard mortgaged an entire draft class to build this roster, and now there's no cushion left if it doesn't work. Bowers, Wilson, and Flowers form a genuinely dangerous young skill-position core, but the running back room is aging and thin, and there's no rookie capital left this year to fix it. The 10-4-to-6-8 slide is the warning sign: this team needs the talent it already has to perform now, because it has nothing coming to bail it out. The right move is aggressive in-season trading rather than waiting on a draft that isn't coming, targeting a real workhorse back from a seller before the season even kicks off.

Strengths

  • Bowers at TE is one of the best building blocks in the league at any position
  • Garrett Wilson and Zay Flowers give this team a genuinely explosive young WR2/WR3 behind Evans
  • Purdy is a stable, efficient starting QB

Weaknesses

  • Zero picks in the 2026 rookie draft, the only team in the league without one
  • Evans is 32 and the rest of the RB room behind the starters is replacement level (Conner, Montgomery both aging or committee backs)
  • 10-4 to 6-8 decline with no incoming young talent to offset it

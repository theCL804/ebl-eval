Where They Stand

Back-to-back 5-9 seasons and the lowest point total in the league both years, 1243.34 followed by an actual decline to 1042.18. That is not just a run of bad luck, it is the profile of a roster genuinely underproducing, most likely because the twelve-receiver logjam is diluting playing time rather than concentrating it behind real difference-makers. This team was a finals participant as recently as 2023, losing to the Chicken Boys, which makes the two-year slide since then even more striking.

This roster sits in the bottom tier of the standings alongside the Chicken Boys and the Factory, but unlike those two, the construction doesn't obviously support a bounce-back on its own. The fix here has to come from actual roster changes this offseason, not from better health or better touchdown luck next season.

Future Outlook

Caleb Williams (24) is a legitimate long-term answer at QB, and Rome Odunze (24) and Efton Chism (24) both have real WR1-caliber trajectories if they get more concentrated volume. That's a fine foundation, the problem is everything else on the roster is either a lottery ticket or a hole.

With a first and three seconds in this class plus a loaded 2028 (six picks, a first among them), the multi-year outlook depends entirely on whether this front office finally uses that capital to add a real running back rather than another speculative receiver. Do that, and Williams-Odunze-Chism is a real core to build 2027 and 2028 around.

Scouting Report

This roster has a real problem: it's hoarding depth at the position it's already deepest at while running dangerously thin at running back. Twelve receivers is a lot of bodies for four to six roster spots, and most of the names past Odunze and Chism are speculative. The fix is straightforward even if it's not fun: package three or four of the fringe WR dart-throws, add in a couple of the extra seconds, and go get a real starting running back. With eight picks in this class, there's no excuse for walking out of the draft without addressing the backfield directly, even if it costs moving up from the back of the first round.

Strengths

  • Eight picks in the 2026 draft (a first and three seconds among them), plenty of ammunition to fix specific holes
  • Caleb Williams gives this team a real long-term QB anchor
  • Odunze and Chism give this team a foundation at WR to build around

Weaknesses

  • Twelve rostered receivers and most of them are lottery tickets (Legette, Bech, Ayomanor, Royals) rather than sure things
  • Only five running backs total and none of them project as a clear lead back
  • Two straight 5-9 seasons and the lowest scoring output in the league both years

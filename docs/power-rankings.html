Ethel's Dynasty: 2026 Draft Preview

Twelve teams, half-PPR, one quarterback, four-round rookie draft. The gap between the top and bottom of this league is mostly about draft capital discipline: the best teams either built deep rosters and kept their future picks intact, or spent capital ruthlessly to win now. The bottom of the standings is stacked with future first-round picks waiting to be turned into a real core. The league's history looms over this draft too: the Chicken Boys' four-peat dynasty has completely collapsed, Long Live Wopo is the only two-time champion, and the Moon Unit enters as defending champs after finally breaking through in 2025.

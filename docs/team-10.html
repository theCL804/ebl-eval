Where They Stand

Matches BateBoul's 12-2 record in both 2024 and 2025, with elite point totals both years as well (1553.26, then 1498.42), placing this roster in the exact same top tier as the league's other juggernaut. The 2024 season ended in a championship-game loss to Long Live Wopo, so this roster has already proven it can get there; it just hasn't closed it out yet.

The difference between the two is how they got there: this roster spent far more of its draft capital assembling the current core, which shows up in how thin the incoming picks are. As long as the RB trio stays healthy, the standing here doesn't change, but there is less margin for error than BateBoul has if any of the top three backs go down, since there isn't much depth behind them and not much draft capital left to fix it in-season.

Future Outlook

TreVeyon Henderson (23) and De'Von Achane (24) are both young enough to anchor a backfield well past 2028, which softens the concern about Jonathan Taylor's timeline somewhat, he's the one member of the trio who'll be aging out first. Jameson Williams (25) is also still ascending at receiver.

The catch is the almost total absence of future draft capital (a single third in 2027, three picks total in 2028), which means there's no easy way to add a fourth long-term piece if injuries hit or if Taylor declines faster than expected. This outlook is good specifically because Henderson and Achane are already secured, not because there's a plan to add more.

Scouting Report

The Choo Choo Crew has been the most dominant team in the league for two seasons running, and the roster shows exactly how: an elite three-headed backfield, a true QB1, and enough perimeter talent to not need to draft. The tradeoff is almost no incoming draft capital, which is fine as long as the timeline is now. The real long-term risk is that Henderson, Taylor, and Achane are all in the same general career window; when that trio starts declining it will likely happen at once, and there's very little behind them to soften the blow. Enjoy the title window, but start using the aging bench pieces (Kupp, Allen, McLaurin) as trade sweeteners now to quietly restock future picks while they still carry value.

Strengths

  • Henderson, Jonathan Taylor, and Achane is arguably the best three-man backfield in the league
  • Burrow at QB with a strong supporting cast (Godwin, Sutton, Jameson Williams)
  • Back-to-back 12-2 seasons, the best sustained record in the league

Weaknesses

  • Minimal 2026 and 2027 draft capital (two picks this year, one in 2027), the price of building this roster
  • Bench is full of aging vets on expiring windows (Kupp, Allen, McLaurin, Cousins) with little long-term value
  • Very little young talent waiting in the wings if the RB trio ages out simultaneously

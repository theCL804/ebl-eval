Where They Stand

Two consecutive 6-8 seasons, squarely in the league's middle class: neither good enough to contend nor bad enough to fully sell. That is often the most dangerous place to sit in a dynasty league, since it invites half-measures instead of a real decision. The one finals appearance in this team's recent history was a loss, to Long Live Wopo back in 2019, and it hasn't been particularly close to another one since.

The roster's average age (26.6) and its top contributors (Henry at 32, Kelce at 36) both point toward a closing window rather than an opening one. This team is nowhere close to the top tier right now, BateBoul, the Choo Choo Crew, and the Moon Unit all finished with 11 or more wins in 2025, and staying in the mushy middle for one more year without spending the stacked draft capital would be the worst possible outcome.

Future Outlook

There's already a young core quietly forming underneath the aging vets: Stroud is 24 and entering his ascending years, Brian Thomas (23), Ladd McConkey (24), and Ricky Pearsall (25) all have real WR1/WR2 trajectories, and Chase Brown (26) is a legitimate every-down back. By 2027 or 2028, that group is fully in its prime at the exact moment Henry, Jones, and Kelce are gone from the league entirely.

The four future first-round picks are the accelerant, not the plan itself. Spend them on a young TE and one more difference-making receiver or back, and this roster quietly becomes Stroud's team well before the current vets retire, rather than needing a slow multi-year teardown.

Scouting Report

This roster is a snapshot of a team that contended a couple of years ago and didn't turn the roster over in time. Henry and Jones were great value buys once, but a dynasty backfield anchored by two players over 30 is a ticking clock, and Kelce at 36 makes the TE room a liability waiting to happen. The saving grace is that this front office clearly saw it coming and hoarded picks: four firsts across the next three classes. That's the resource to go get a young workhorse back and a real TE in the same offseason. Sell Henry and Kelce now while they still have redraft value, and spend the stack of firsts rebuilding the skill-position core around Stroud rather than trying to squeeze one more year out of the current group.

Strengths

  • Two firsts in 2026, two firsts in 2027, and a first in 2028 is as much ammunition as anyone not named The Factory
  • Stroud at QB is a legitimate long-term answer
  • Deep, if speculative, QB stash (Daniels, Wilson, Tua) gives trade flexibility in a one-QB league

Weaknesses

  • Henry (32) and Jones (31) are the starting backfield, an age profile that does not survive to the next contention window
  • Kelce at 36 is one bad training camp from irrelevance and there is no succession plan at TE
  • Back-to-back 6-8 seasons with an aging core is the exact setup for a fast decline

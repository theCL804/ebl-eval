Where They Stand

The best regular-season standing in the league by a clear margin: 12-2 and 11-3 in back-to-back years, and the most points scored in the league both seasons, 1622.56 followed by 1658.66. No other roster has combined that level of volume and consistency over two straight years, but the 2025 season ended in a finals loss to the Moon Unit, the closest this team has come to a title and still short.

Heading into this draft, this is the team every other roster is either trying to catch or trying to avoid meeting in the semifinals. The only real question is whether they can get better at the margins, since there isn't an obvious hole left to fill on this roster.

Future Outlook

This is the rare contender whose best players are also its youngest: Bucky Irving (23), Ja'Marr Chase (26), Drake London (25), and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (24) are all either still improving or squarely in their prime, and Trey McBride (26) is one of the best young TEs in the league. Almost none of that core needs replacing in the next three years.

That's what makes this roster's outlook different from the other win-now teams in the league: BateBoul isn't racing a closing window, the current core should still be excellent in 2028. The only real long-term need is finding a young QB to eventually replace Dak, and multiple future seconds give them the ammunition to go get one without disturbing anything else.

Scouting Report

BateBoul has been the best team in the league for two straight years and the roster explains why: elite WR depth, a true difference-making TE, and enough RB competence to not be a liability. There isn't much left to fix, which is a good problem to have. The one real crack is QB, where Dak is solid but unspectacular and there's no young answer waiting behind him. With multiple seconds in this draft and a full 2027/2028 complement, the highest-value move is packaging picks to jump into the back of the first round for a rookie QB with real upside, insurance for the day Dak's play (or health) turns.

Strengths

  • 12-2 in 2025 on top of 11-3 in 2024, the most consistent winning roster in the league
  • Chase, Deebo, London, and Smith-Njigba is an elite four-man WR room
  • McBride is a legitimate top-tier TE, a spot most of this league is scrambling to fill

Weaknesses

  • No 2026 first-round pick, the cost of maintaining a roster this stacked
  • QB room is thin on immediate upside behind Dak (Flacco is a 41-year-old emergency option, Hurts is a stashed handcuff at best)
  • Very little separation left to create since the roster is already near its ceiling
